# Graduate Research Interests - Yohannes Alelign Biresaw

My central research interest is **secure and trustworthy intelligent systems**. I am interested in how machine-learning models can remain reliable when data are sensitive, distributed, imbalanced, heterogeneous, or exposed to security threats. This broad theme connects my interests in artificial intelligence and machine learning, cybersecurity, applied cryptography, privacy-preserving computation, and secure distributed systems.

## Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

Robust learning, trustworthy AI, model calibration, generalization, uncertainty, and careful evaluation. My current portfolio uses federated medical imaging as a concrete case study, but the underlying questions apply to many real-world intelligent systems.

## Cybersecurity and AI Security

The use of machine learning for cybersecurity and the security of machine-learning systems themselves, including anomaly detection, adversarial robustness, data poisoning, model integrity, privacy leakage, and secure deployment.

## Cryptography and Privacy-Preserving Computing

Applied cryptography and privacy-preserving mechanisms that support collaboration without exposing sensitive information, including secure aggregation, cryptographic protocols for distributed learning, and trade-offs between security, privacy, communication, and model utility.

## Graduate Objective

I hope to join a research group where I can focus on one well-defined problem aligned with the advisor's expertise while developing stronger foundations in mathematics, algorithms, security, and experimental research.
