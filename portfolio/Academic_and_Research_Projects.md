## 1. Federated Medical-Imaging Research Project

**Evaluating FedAvg and FedProx for Federated Classification of Pneumonia-Associated Lung Opacity Under Non-IID Data**  
**Completed Research Project | 2026**

Developed and executed a patient-aware research pipeline comparing centralized training, FedAvg, and FedProx under IID and controlled non-IID conditions. The project includes DICOM preprocessing, patient-exclusive splitting, five-client federated simulations, three random seeds, validation-only model selection, client-drift analysis, a frozen final-test protocol, and reproducible research documentation.

**Main finding:** Increasing heterogeneity reduced federated performance, especially PR-AUC. FedProx substantially reduced client and global update magnitudes and achieved slightly higher balanced accuracy and F1-score, while FedAvg retained a small PR-AUC advantage. The results show that stronger optimization stability does not automatically produce stronger predictive ranking.

**Repository:** https://github.com/john121319/federated-pneumonia-research
