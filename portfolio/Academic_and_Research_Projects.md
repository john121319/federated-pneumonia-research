# Academic and Research Projects - Yohannes Alelign Biresaw

## Federated medical-imaging research

**FedAvg and FedProx Under Controlled Non-IID Client Heterogeneity for Pneumonia-Associated Lung-Opacity Classification**

Completed a reproducible federated-learning study using 26,684 RSNA chest-radiograph examinations linked to 11,452 original patients. Developed patient-exclusive train, validation, and test splits; created IID and controlled non-IID client partitions; compared centralized learning, FedAvg, and FedProx across three random seeds; and applied validation-only selection before one frozen final test evaluation. The study found that FedProx substantially reduced client drift, while neither FedAvg nor FedProx consistently dominated across all predictive metrics.
